This derivative was optimized from the authorized GLB already present in this project.
Changes: geometry simplification (target 15%, error 0.002); deduplication and welding;
resampled animations; maximum 512 px textures compressed to WebP; Meshopt geometry
and animation compression. The three original animation clips, skeleton and embedded
source/license metadata are retained. Original 500,419,044 bytes; derivative 4,900,616 bytes.

In-game scale: 1.85 m per character. Four skeleton-safe instances share geometry and
materials. Three play fictional visual stand-ins for Neil Armstrong, Michael Collins,
and Buzz Aldrin. This science-fiction character is an artistic adaptation, not an
accurate reconstruction of Apollo A7L pressure suits. No historical dialogue is
attributed to these visual stand-ins. Some attached cargo/crate meshes are hidden.

Rocket and launch infrastructure: original project-authored geometry. This asset does
not contain the Sketchfab LEGO Saturn V model and does not extract it from a viewer.
